Make the 13 mini last.

Settings, charging habits and automations for the iPhone 13 mini, from five-minute fixes to deeper tuning. The battery in the corner fills as you read.

Menu names can shift between iOS versions. This guide was written on iOS 27. If a setting isn't where it says, use the search bar in Settings. No single tip is dramatic on its own: battery life comes from small savings adding up.

Basics

Free settings anyone can change.

Check your battery health first

Settings > Battery > Battery Health & Charging

Maximum Capacity shows how much of the original capacity is left. If it has fallen well below 80%, no setting fully fixes it, and a battery replacement is the real solution.

Use Dark Mode and a dark wallpaper

Settings > Display & Brightness > Dark

The 13 mini has an OLED display, where black pixels use almost no power. It helps whenever the screen is on, but it won't transform battery life on its own.

Set cellular to 5G Auto

Settings > Cellular > Cellular Data Options > Voice & Data

5G can use more power than LTE, especially with a weak signal. 5G Auto uses 5G only when it's worth the battery cost. If your 5G coverage is poor, LTE may save more.

Tighten Location Services

Settings > Privacy & Security > Location Services

Set apps to While Using or Never unless they truly need background access, like navigation. Turn off Precise Location for apps that only need your general area.

Other quick wins

  • Turn on Auto-Brightness in Accessibility > Display & Text Size.
  • Turn off Raise to Wake if the screen lights up when you don't need it.
  • Trim notifications. Each one wakes the screen.
  • Prefer Wi-Fi over cellular when it's available.
  • Use Low Power Mode when the battery runs low.

Charging habits

Slowing down battery aging.

Leave Optimized Battery Charging on

Settings > Battery > Battery Health & Charging

It's on by default. It learns your routine and reduces the time the phone sits fully charged, while aiming for 100% when you usually unplug.

The 13 mini has no true charge limit

The hard 80% Charge Limit exists only on iPhone 15 and newer. If your 13 mini says it's charging to 80%, that's Optimized Battery Charging pausing there and finishing to 100% later. It doesn't stop permanently.

Optional: unplug around 80%

Lower charge levels put less stress on a lithium-ion cell, so this may reduce wear. The trade-off is less battery on hand, and how much it helps in practice is debated. Treat it as an optional habit.

Keep it cool

Heat is the biggest enemy of the battery.

  • Don't charge under a pillow, in a hot car, or in direct sun.
  • Take the case off if the phone feels warm while charging.

Consider slower charging

Faster chargers make more heat, and heat ages the battery. A slow 5W charger for routine or overnight charging keeps the phone cooler. Keep a faster one for days you need a quick top-up.

Avoid draining to 0%

Regularly running the battery flat is harder on it than shallower cycles. Topping up during the day is fine.

Advanced

Background tuning and automations.

Reduce background activity

Apps that refresh in the background wake the phone and use power.

  • Settings > General > Background App Refresh: turn it off for apps that don't need it, or limit it to Wi-Fi.
  • Settings > Mail > Mail Accounts > Fetch New Data: Push can be efficient where supported. For Fetch accounts, a longer interval or Manually uses the least power, with delayed mail as the cost.

Automate Low Power Mode with Shortcuts

Shortcuts has no true "phone is idle" trigger. Use one of these instead: Sleep Focus, Time of Day, Battery Level (for example, below 30%), or Charger.

  1. Open Shortcuts > Automation > New Automation.
  2. Choose a trigger.
  3. Add the action Set Low Power Mode > On.
  4. Choose Run Immediately so it doesn't ask each time.

iOS turns Low Power Mode off by itself once the phone charges past about 80%, so charging can override the automation.

Doesn't apply to the 13 mini: the built-in 80% Charge Limit slider (iPhone 15 and newer only).

Do these first

  • Check Battery Health.
  • Dark Mode plus a dark wallpaper.
  • Cellular on 5G Auto.
  • Location Services on While Using or Never.
  • Background App Refresh off for apps that don't need it.
  • Keep the phone cool while charging.
